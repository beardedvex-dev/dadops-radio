# Dad Net: test night checklist

Grab 2–4 people on a casual night (Motor Pool Monday is perfect). Use
headphones so the radio doesn't echo into your mic. Everyone keeps using
Discord as normal at the same time.

Send them the radio address and this:

> Open this in Chrome or Edge on your PC (or your phone), sign in with the
> same Discord you use on dadops.gg, click **Get on net**, allow the mic,
> then click **Join net** on Recon. If you're in a team channel in
> Discord, click **Take FTL** to talk on Command.

## The checks

Tick each one, and note anything odd.

**Getting on**
- [ ] Everyone gets to **On net** without errors
- [ ] A Recruit (if one's around) sees Command, Recon, Air Support and Logistics as "opens at
      Private" and can only listen to All-call

**FTL seats**
- [ ] Two people sit in different "Fireteam" channels in Discord. Each sees
      their fireteam in the FIRETEAMS box within ~15 seconds, with **Take FTL**
- [ ] Take FTL puts them on Command in Talk mode; the other person sees "Name · FT1 FTL"
- [ ] A Private without a seat (and below Sergeant) only listens on Command
- [ ] Tonight's Squad Leader can assign, change and clear a seat from the dropdown
- [ ] An FTL leaves their Discord channel: the seat shows (away), then frees itself
- [ ] **Go off net** then **Get back on net** works, and the Battle Board stays open

**Command**
- [ ] Two FTLs (or Sergeants) are on Command; one holds the talk button (or the key with the
      radio tab in front). The other hears them, in the **left ear**
- [ ] The card shows "<name> is talking" while they talk, and it clears when they stop
- [ ] Someone **not** on Command hears nothing
- [ ] A short chirp plays when someone starts talking
- [ ] The voice sounds clear: not robotic, not choppy, and the first word
      isn't cut off

**Talking over Discord**
- [ ] Talk in Discord and on Command at the same time from different people.
      Can you follow both?
- [ ] Try the ear setting on **Both** vs **Left**. Which do the leads prefer?

**All-call**
- [ ] You (S3) hold the All-call button: everyone on the radio hears it, in both ears
- [ ] Someone who isn't S3 or tonight's Squad Leader has no All-call button

**Recon, Air Support, Logistics**
- [ ] Two people join Recon (then Air Support, then Logistics); each plays in the **right ear** and doesn't
      show up on Command

**Networks**
- [ ] Everyone checks the line under their name: **Voice: direct** or
      **Voice: backup route (relay)**. Note who's on the backup route and what
      network they're on. Both should sound the same.

**Direct line**
- [ ] Click someone's name on a net, hold the Direct key: only they hear you
- [ ] They hold their Direct key without picking anyone: it answers you
- [ ] The Next key steps through people; the overlay shows 📞 and the name

**Turn Discord down (app)**
- [ ] With Discord in a call, someone talks on Dad Net: Discord drops, then comes back
- [ ] Try muted and 30%. Which do the leads like?

**Staying on**
- [ ] Leave the tab open for 30+ minutes. Still On net, still works?
- [ ] Turn Wi-Fi off and on again on one device. Does it come back by itself?

## Afterwards, tell Claude

- What worked and what didn't
- Anyone who couldn't hear or be heard (and if they were on Wi-Fi, a work/
  hotel network, or a phone on mobile data)
- Whether the leads liked one ear or both ears
- Anything the desktop app should do differently
