DAD NET

Limited Availability

DAD Ops' own radio. Talk to the other fireteam leads without leaving your squad's Discord channel. Take your fireteam's FTL seat, hold a key, reach every lead on Command, and keep playing.

Download for Windows Open in your browser

Free. Windows 10/11. Sign in with the same Discord you use on dadops.gg. The browser version works on Mac and phones too (keys only while its tab is in front).

How it works

Discord for your squad. The radio for everyone else.

Stay in your channel

Your fireteam keeps talking in its Discord channel with your normal Discord push-to-talk. Nothing about that changes.

Take the FTL seat

Each Discord fireteam channel gets one FTL seat. Click Take FTL, hold your Command key (say Mouse 5) and every lead hears you, in one ear. Your squad doesn't hear the chatter.

See who's talking

A small overlay in the corner shows who's on which net, with their seat: Pickles · FT1 FTL. Put it in any corner, any size. Press F9 for the whole field kit.

Setup · about 5 minutes

Get on net

1

Install

Download, run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new: click More info → Run anyway.

2

Sign in

Sign in with Discord (the same account as dadops.gg). It gets on net by itself; allow the microphone if Windows asks.

3

Take a seat, set your keys

Leading a fireteam? Click Take FTL by your Discord channel. Then click Command's key box and press the button you want. Use a mouse button or a key you never type with: talk keys work everywhere, even while you type.

4

Game in Borderless

Set WARDOGS to Borderless or Windowed so the overlay and the field kit can show on top. Nothing can draw over exclusive Fullscreen.

The field kit

Radio and Battle Board, one device

F9 anywhere

Pops the kit up over the game with the Battle Board folded out: tonight's op and countdown, the squad board with every fireteam and FTL, your comms, a stopwatch, a countdown timer and a notepad. Press it again to tuck it away.

Fold it

Just want the radio? ◂ FOLD on the hinge. Want the board back? The BATTLE BOARD ▸ tab on the edge. Need a break? Go off net and the kit stays open.

Lives in the tray

× hides it by the clock and keeps you on net. It can start with Windows, and it updates itself.

Questions

Good to know

Does it touch the game? Anti-cheat?

No. The overlay is its own see-through window on top, like Discord's, and the talk keys use the same standard Windows key listener Discord and OBS use. Nothing reads or changes the game.

My talk key types into chat / keys me up while typing

Talk keys work everywhere, so pick something you never type with: Mouse 5, Mouse 4, a numpad key or F13. New installs start on Mouse 5 (Command) and Mouse 4 (All-call).

Talk keys don't work in the game
  • If the game (or its launcher) runs as administrator, Windows won't pass its keys to a normal app. Try running the radio as administrator once to check.
  • The Desktop app drawer at the bottom of the radio says whether in-game keys are working.
The overlay or the kit doesn't show over the game

Switch the game to Borderless or Windowed. Also check Show the overlay in-game is ticked in the Desktop app drawer.

"Windows protected your PC" or an antivirus warning

The app isn't code-signed yet, and new apps that listen for keys sometimes get flagged. Click More info → Run anyway. The code is public on GitHub.

It says On net, but nobody can hear me (or I hear nobody)

Some networks (work, hotels, some phone data) block the normal way voice travels. Dad Net switches to a backup route through Cloudflare's relay by itself. The line under your name says Voice: direct or Voice: backup route. If it still doesn't work, tell an S3 what network you're on.

Mac, Linux or phone?

The desktop app is Windows only. The browser version works anywhere, with keys while its tab is in front and hold-to-talk buttons.

Who can use it?

DAD Ops members signed in on dadops.gg. Recruits can listen to All-call; everything else opens at Private. Talking on Command takes your fireteam's FTL seat or Sergeant and up.

How do FTL seats work?
  • Every team channel in Discord gets one FTL seat: the ones Horton opens when the teams go out (like ⚔️ Assault 1), any squad channel next to The Rally Point, and anything named Fireteam 1 or FT1. Anyone Private+ sitting in that channel can take it.
  • Tonight's Squad Leader and S3 can assign, change or clear seats.
  • Leave your fireteam's channel and the seat frees itself after about a minute. Seats reset every op night.
Where did the Field PDA go?

It's still on dadops.gg. The app now folds out the Battle Board, built for the op itself, with a link to the full PDA at the bottom.
